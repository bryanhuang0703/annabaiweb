04 · About

Infrastructure roots, hardware experience.

Annabai Tech was founded in Beijing by Bryan Huang. Before that, Bryan spent more than ten years working with the global teams of Google, Zynga, LinkedIn and Airbnb to build their China corporate infrastructure, twice supporting a China team from 10 to 300 people.

He has since led hardware products from concept to mass production. As co-founder of a motorized fitness company, he defined the product, built the team and managed partners across the motor and its embedded firmware, a custom control board from circuit design to PCBA, mechanical structure and industrial design.

Earlier, he worked on the software and motor supply chain for a smart spin bike at a Xiaomi ecosystem company, and helped improve the identity hardware of a Silicon Valley startup. We bring both sides to every project: how systems are built, and how they are made.
